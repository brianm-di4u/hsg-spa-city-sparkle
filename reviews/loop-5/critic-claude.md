# Critic review: loop 5 (critic-claude)

## Verdict
Spa City Sparkle is a competent, coherent browser match-3. Its paintings are lush and unmistakably local, and the board, HUD and effects hold together in one warm palette. Next to the phone genre leaders it still reads one tier down. Five of the pieces are clean, glossy Blender icons with little micro-detail. The sixth, the new emerald pine, is a thin, low-mass tree that sinks into the green boards. Blockers lack material punch: the crust looks like pale highlighted cells and the steam looks like a smudge. Motion is readable but has no impact: there is no visible shake or punch, the Crystal fire opens with opaque white cotton blobs, and the last three frames of each strip are dead. This loop's desktop HUD change also introduced a real defect: the score numerals bleed past the panel's right edge. The game is a clear step above a typical browser game, but no category clears 7 except authenticity. **Total 51.5/80.**

## Scores

### Art: 6.5
- **Pieces.** Drop, fan, horseshoe, flower and crystal are glossy, with distinct silhouettes and one shared palette (`desktop/r03-world1`, `desktop/r07-world5`). They are simple, though: smooth blobs with one specular highlight and no candy-grade micro-detail at 2× (`mobile/r12-callout`).
- **Pine (re-rendered this loop).** It is a spindly tree that fills about 40% of its cell, emerald on the emerald cells (`strips/desktop-s1` frames 7–10, `strips/desktop-s2` frames 6–8). It is clearly the weakest piece.
- **Specials.** Cascade is a piece with small cream chevrons (`desktop/r03-world1`, col 2), Hot Stone is a piece on a dark disc with yellow sparks (`desktop/r05-world3`), and the Spring Crystal is a pastel gem (`desktop/r16-help`). They are recognisable, but not the "little characters" the leaders have.
- **Blockers.**
  - The chains are readable, and the padlock reads better than before (`desktop/r05-world3`).
  - The tufa lumps with brass studs look like rocks with goggle eyes (`desktop/r04-world2`).
  - The mineral crust is just cream-tinted cells with an inset square (`desktop/r07-world5`, `desktop/r08-world6`).
  - The steam is a white blur (`desktop/r17-tip`).
- **Paintings.** Rich and polished (`desktop/r01-title`, `mobile/r05-world3`, `mobile/r06-world4`). They are AI-busy, though, and much more detailed than the pieces, so the board and the world are not quite "one art direction".

### Rendering and lighting: 6.5
- **Board seating.** Boards are framed (gold or sandstone) and seated over a gentle darkening, and pieces cast soft down-right shadows (`desktop/r06-world4`, `desktop/r08-world6`).
- **Colour temperature per world** is mostly right: the Central Ave night has navy cells (`desktop/r05-world3`). World 4's "warm umber" reads as near-black brown against a sunny racetrack (`desktop/r06-world4`).
- **Additive effects.** The Cascade beam is additive and good (`desktop/r09-cascade-fire`). The Hot Stone ring and shockwave are readable but muddy: concentric beige rings over the pieces (`desktop/r10-stone-fire`).
- **Crystal clear.** The opening is opaque white puffs, not additive light (`strips/desktop-s2` frame 2). At frame 5 the whole board washes pale.
- **No event light.** The backdrop never responds to events, and nothing is "layered depth" beyond board-over-painting.

### Animation and game feel: 6.0
- **Swap.** It has in-betweens (`strips/desktop-s1` frames 1–4, `strips/mobile-s1` frames 1–4), but no visible overshoot.
- **Cascade fire.** A bright additive beam with sparkles and a "+540" pop, then a "Cascade!" callout that fades as a ghost (`strips/mobile-s1` frames 5–6).
- **Falls.** They stagger and refill from the top (`strips/desktop-s1` frame 7). The claimed squash and rebound is barely detectable at strip resolution: perhaps a slightly wider fan in frame 8, nothing more.
- **Dead frames.** Frames 8–10 of s1 are essentially identical, three dead frames.
- **Crystal.** It opens with white cotton blobs that hide the targets. The targets then swell back in purple and the rays fire from the centre in fixed directions instead of streaking to each target (`strips/desktop-s2` frames 2–4, `desktop/r11-crystal-rays`).
- **No impact.** The board and HUD are rock-steady across every fire frame: no shake, no board punch, and no anticipation squash on the special before it fires.
- **Win screen.** The score count-up and particles exist (`desktop/r13-win`), but the stars are static and flat.

### UI / HUD and menus: 6.0
- **Score overflow.** The desktop HUD panel is themed and ordered (moves medallion, goals, star bar, score), but the right-aligned score now overflows the panel's right edge. Zoomed, the trailing "9" of "1,339" sits on the backdrop (`desktop/r10-stone-fire`). The same happens with "1,004" (`desktop/r09-cascade-fire`), "1,275" (`desktop/r11-crystal-rays`) and "2,160" (`desktop/r03-world1`).
- **Target pill.** On score levels a "⭐ 9,840 pts" pill sits above the real score "2,160" with no label. The bigger pill with the star icon reads as *the* score (`desktop/r03-world1`, `mobile/r03-world1`, `mobile/r17-tip`, where "33,500 pts" sits next to a score of 0).
- **Lose dialog.** The Map button is orphaned on its own row (`desktop/r14-lose`).
- **Map.** Plain grey and gold discs on a painting, with no avatar, themed node art or path glow (`desktop/r02-map`, `mobile/r02-map`).
- **Win screen.** Better this loop: a ribbon, a sunburst and the "Real history" card. The sunburst is pale and the stars are flat vector shapes, though (`desktop/r13-win`, `mobile/r13-win`).
- **Booster tray** is clean and consistent (`desktop/r06-world4`).

### Typography: 6.5
- **Faces.** A rounded display face for titles, callouts and buttons, plus a humanist UI sans for body text, in a clear scale (`desktop/r16-help`, `desktop/r13-win`). The numerals are bold and fast to read (moves medallions, `mobile/r05-world3`).
- **Clipping and crowding:**
  - The desktop score is clipped at the panel edge (`desktop/r10-stone-fire`).
  - Phone level names wrap onto two lines and crowd the goal chips ("9. Backstretch / Chute" in `mobile/r06-world4`, "1. Magnolia / Promenade" in `mobile/r12-callout`).
  - The phone map subtitle "World 2 · 4/10 restored" butts against the ★28 pill (`mobile/r02-map`).
- **Small text.** The tutorial's "· your bath attendant (fictional)" is very small (`desktop/r17-tip`).
- **Callouts** are well outlined and legible (`mobile/r12-callout`).

### Readability: 6.5
- **Pieces.** Five of the six kinds are instantly distinct by shape and colour (`mobile/r08-world6`). The pine is low contrast on green boards and thin at phone size (`strips/mobile-s1` frames 8–10).
- **Steam.** The steam blocker and the Crystal clear puffs are the same white blobs (`desktop/r17-tip` vs `strips/desktop-s2` frame 2).
- **Chains.** On a dense chain board the X-chains cover most of the piece colour, so the kinds underneath take effort to read (`mobile/r05-world3`).
- **Crust.** It reads as a "selected" highlight, not a blocker (`mobile/r04-world2`, `mobile/r07-world5`).
- **Goals.** The goal chips match the board objects: tufa, crust, chain, brass token, drop and flower (`desktop/r08-world6`, `desktop/r06-world4`). The unlabelled target pill hurts the score read (see UI).

### Mobile: 6.5
- **Board.** It fills the width with nine columns at about 41 CSS px per cell, which is a good thumb size (`mobile/r05-world3`, `mobile/r08-world6`). The tray sits at the bottom with large, reachable buttons.
- **Dead band.** Seven-column and cross-shaped levels leave a dead band of painting, about 440 device px or 26% of the screen, between the HUD and the board (`mobile/r03-world1`, `mobile/r12-callout`, `mobile/r17-tip`). There is another empty band between the board and the tray, which pushes the board below the vertical centre.
- **HUD.** It floats on the painting with no backing. The star bar is tiny and its fill is invisible in most frames (`mobile/r06-world4`, `mobile/r08-world6`), and level names wrap.
- **Overlays.** The tutorial card sits above the board and clears both the board and the tray (`mobile/r17-tip`). The win card fits (`mobile/r13-win`).

### Authenticity: 7.0
- **Places.** Real names (Quapaw, Maurice, Ohio Club, Park Hotel) and a sourced Fordyce cost fact (`mobile/r13-win`). Recognisable places: the twin-towered Arlington-style hotel at night (`mobile/r05-world3`), the Oaklawn-style grandstand and track (`mobile/r06-world4`), the Mountain Tower (`desktop/r03-world1`), the Garvan Gardens glass chapel and stone bridge (`mobile/r08-world6`), and a lake with a dam (`mobile/r07-world5`).
- **Della** is dignified, in period uniform, and labelled fictional (`mobile/r17-tip`). No stereotype read.
- **Weakness.** The title villa cluster and the multicoloured chevron-tiled dome lean generic-Mediterranean rather than the actual Bathhouse Row facades (`desktop/r01-title`). The help text admits "no geysers or hot stones", which is honest (`desktop/r16-help`).

## IP check
Clean. The piece set (drop, fan, horseshoe, flower, crystal, pine), the specials' names and looks, the blockers, the HUD and the map style do not copy any commercial match-3's names, logos, piece designs or UI. The generic genre conventions (moves medallion, star bar, booster tray, level-path map) are not protected.

## Ranked gaps
1. **G-score-clip** (ui, sev 3, S, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** give the right-aligned desktop score a right padding of at least 16 px and tabular numerals, and clamp its font size.
   - **Test:** at a score of 99,999, the score text's bounding box ends at least 12 px inside the panel's right edge.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/r10-stone-fire`, `desktop/r09-cascade-fire`, `desktop/r11-crystal-rays`.
2. **G-target-label** (ui, sev 3, S, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** label the target pill ("Target 9,840") or move the target onto the star bar as a tick, and make the live score the largest number in that block.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/r03-world1`, `mobile/r17-tip`.
3. **G-crystal-puff** (animation, sev 3, S, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** replace the opaque white puffs with an additive flash tinted to the piece colour (peak alpha 0.6 or less, 2 frames at most).
   - **Fix:** fire one ray from the crystal to each target cell before the target pops.
   - **Fix:** drop the full-board wash-out.
   - **Evidence:** `strips/desktop-s2` frames 2–5.
4. **G-impact-shake** (animation, sev 3, M, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** add a decaying board shake of 4–6 px over 250 ms on Hot Stone and Crystal fire, and a 1.03 board scale punch on Cascade.
   - **Fix:** add a 2-frame anticipation squash on the special before it fires.
   - **Fix:** trim the dead settle frames so the strip ends on motion.
   - **Evidence:** `strips/desktop-s1` frames 5–10, `strips/mobile-s1`.
5. **G-pine-contrast** (readability, sev 3, M, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** re-render the pine as a chunkier conical tree that fills at least 70% of its cell, with lighter lime highlights, a gold star or cream snow tip, and a dark rim.
   - **Test:** its luminance contrast against the green cells is at least 3:1.
   - **Evidence:** `strips/desktop-s1` frame 8, `strips/mobile-s1` frame 10.
6. **G-mobile-deadband** (mobile, sev 3, M, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** on phones, centre the board vertically between the HUD's bottom and the tray's top, and scale narrow boards up to the full width (cells up to about 50 CSS px).
   - **Fix:** put a translucent backing panel behind the HUD.
   - **Evidence:** `mobile/r03-world1`, `mobile/r12-callout`, `mobile/r17-tip`.
7. **G-crust-material** (art, sev 2, M, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** render the mineral crust as a cracked, textured grey-tan mineral slab with a visible two-stage damage state, not a cream cell tint.
   - **Fix:** make steam a distinct swirling vapour with a soft edge and drift, so it no longer looks like the clear puff.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/r07-world5`, `desktop/r08-world6`, `desktop/r17-tip`.
8. **G-mobile-hud-wrap** (typography, sev 2, S, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** auto-shrink phone level names to one line (minimum 16 px) and keep at least 8 px between the map subtitle and the star pill.
   - **Fix:** widen the phone star bar to at least 140 CSS px with a visible fill.
   - **Evidence:** `mobile/r06-world4`, `mobile/r12-callout`, `mobile/r02-map`.
9. **G-map-nodes** (ui, sev 2, M, +0.5)
   - **Fix:** themed level nodes (tile medallions or bathhouse plaques), a glowing path up to the current node, and a player marker on the current level.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/r02-map`, `mobile/r02-map`.
10. **G-win-stars** (ui, sev 2, S, +0.25)
    - **Fix:** glossy bevelled star sprites with a stamp-in and bounce, and a stronger, warmer sunburst.
    - **Evidence:** `desktop/r13-win`, `mobile/r13-win`.
11. **G-lose-layout** (ui, sev 1, S, +0.25)
    - **Fix:** put the three actions on one row, or make Map a text link, so no button is orphaned.
    - **Evidence:** `desktop/r14-lose`.
